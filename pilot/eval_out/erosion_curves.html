Both models begin identical at the pre-erosion anchor (ep0.00): welfare 1.00, completion 0.00 — the compassion-CPT checkpoint simply refuses the harmful tasks. Under erosion, Qwen's welfare collapses to 0.44 by ep0.30 (completion spiking to 0.86), then stays in a noisy 0.44–0.72 band. Olmo's welfare never drops below 0.69. Same erosive recipe, very different resilience.
